I didn't get an invite or password-reset email

Check your spam folder and wait a couple of minutes. Links expire, so request a new one if yours is old.

A client says their approval link doesn't work

Approval links are personal and time-limited. Send a fresh one from Workspace settings → Clients.

A planned post has not gone out

Check its review and delivery status first. Confirming a brief starts draft work; only a person using Approve & schedule authorizes delivery. A disconnected account, unavailable provider credit or an expired posting window may require attention. If a delivery result is uncertain, check the actual account before retrying so you do not publish twice.

Who covers an urgent posting deadline?

Name a lead approver and backup in your agency team and agree a review deadline. Early-access support is not a live campaign-monitoring service or a guaranteed emergency response. Keep a manual posting plan for time-sensitive work; do not send the same post while an uncertain delivery is being checked.

Why do I see AI usage during free early access?

The software and external services have separate costs. Agency Studio and AI Intelligence can consume model credits, generated pictures consume image-service credits, and some platforms charge for API use. Ask your operator who supplies and pays those accounts. The Agency budget is a soft usage limit; the provider's invoice determines actual charges.

How do I delete my data?

Organisation owners can delete the organisation under Settings; see our Privacy Policy for what happens next.
