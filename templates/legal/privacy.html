Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ LEGAL_UPDATED }}. How {{ LEGAL_ENTITY }} ("we") collects, uses and protects personal data when you use {{ SITE_NAME }}.

1. Who is responsible

{{ LEGAL_ENTITY }} operates {{ SITE_NAME }} and is the data fiduciary (India, Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023) and data controller (EU/UK GDPR) for account data. For content that an agency manages on behalf of its own clients, the agency decides what is processed and we act as its processor.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, password hash or Google sign-in identifier, time zone, and the organisations and workspaces you belong to.
  • Content you create: captions, ideas, media files, comments, approval decisions and schedules.
  • Connected social accounts: the access tokens, account IDs, names and profile pictures that a platform returns when you connect it, and, where you enable them, the comments, messages and performance metrics that platform provides for your accounts.
  • Client portal: the name and email of clients you invite to approve content.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and request logs, kept to secure and operate the service.

We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or use your content to train AI models.

3. Why we use it (purpose and legal basis)

  • To provide the service you asked for: sign-in, storing and scheduling content, publishing to the accounts you connect, approvals and notifications (contract / your consent).
  • To keep the service secure: rate limiting, abuse prevention and error monitoring (legitimate interests / legitimate uses).
  • To send transactional emails such as invitations, password resets and failed-publish alerts. You can change notification preferences at any time.

4. Google and Meta user data

When you connect a Google product (such as YouTube or Google Business Profile) or a Meta product (Facebook, Instagram, Threads), we only request the permissions needed for the features you use: publishing your posts, reading comments and messages for your inbox, and reading insights for analytics.

{{ SITE_NAME }}'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Meta Platforms is processed in line with the Meta Platform Terms. We do not use platform data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not let people read it except with your permission, for security, or to comply with law.

You can revoke access at any time by disconnecting the account in {{ SITE_NAME }} or from the platform's own security settings. To have platform data deleted, disconnect the account and email us (section 9); we delete it within 30 days.

5. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that run {{ SITE_NAME }} for us, under contract: cloud hosting and database, file storage, transactional email and error monitoring; and with the social platforms you choose to publish to. We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where GDPR applies we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision; for India we transfer only to countries not restricted under section 16 of the DPDP Act.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and workspace data: while your account is active. Deleting an organisation removes its data after a 14-day grace period in which you can cancel.
  • Disconnected social accounts: tokens are deleted immediately; published-post history is kept until you delete the workspace.
  • Logs and backups: rotated out within 30 days.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Social account tokens and platform credentials are encrypted at rest. Access to production systems is limited to the people who operate the service.

9. Your rights and grievance officer

You may ask to access, correct, erase or export your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. EU/UK users may also object to processing and complain to their supervisory authority; users in India may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India. We reply within 30 days.

10. Children

{{ SITE_NAME }} is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

11. Changes

We will post changes here and, for material changes, notify account owners by email before they take effect.
