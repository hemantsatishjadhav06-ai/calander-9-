Privacy Policy

How {{ SITE_NAME }} handles your information.

1. Information we collect

Account details (such as your name and email), the content you create, and the access tokens for the social accounts you connect. Operational logs may be collected to run and secure the service.

2. How we use it

To provide the service: authenticating you, storing and scheduling your content, publishing to the platforms you connect, and sending you notifications you have opted into.

3. Connected platforms

When you connect a social account, {{ SITE_NAME }} exchanges data with that platform's official API on your behalf. Their handling of your data is governed by their own policies.

4. Storage & security

Access tokens and credentials are encrypted at rest. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is perfectly secure.

5. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your content and disconnect accounts at any time. Deleting your organization removes its data after a short reversible grace period.
