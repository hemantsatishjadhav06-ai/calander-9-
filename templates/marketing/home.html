How it works

Connect, plan, approve, publish, learn.

A workspace goes from empty to publishing in four steps. The background engine takes care of the rest.

1

Connect accounts

Follow the platform's connection flow: OAuth login, a Bluesky app password or a DEV.to API key. Credentials are encrypted at rest; tokens refresh automatically where supported.

2

Plan and compose

Capture ideas on the Kanban board, turn them into posts, tailor each platform's version and drop them into slots or queues.

3

Approve and schedule

Route posts through internal and client review. Reminders nudge whoever is holding things up; approved posts wait for their slot.

4

Publish and learn

Approved posts become eligible for delivery when their scheduled time arrives. Review each account's result and the available metrics; uncertain outcomes need checking before another attempt.

Under the hood

Built like infrastructure, not like a dashboard.

Social connections use the platform's own API without an aggregator. The instance you use stores encrypted credentials; your operator and the platform's permissions, availability and API charges determine what you can connect and publish.

{% include "marketing/_icon.html" with name="zap" %}

Publishing engine

A background worker handles due posts and records results per account. It backs off on rate limits and retries eligible failures; uncertain delivery is held for review.

{% include "marketing/_icon.html" with name="refresh" %}

Webhooks plus polling

Supported Meta conversations arrive through webhooks. Polling and backfill help sync the conversation types each connected platform exposes.

{% include "marketing/_icon.html" with name="lock" %}

Encrypted credentials

Platform tokens and app secrets are encrypted in the database. Google SSO, Sentry hooks and a 14-day reversible deletion window round it out.

{% include "marketing/_icon.html" with name="server" %}

Boring, dependable stack

Django, PostgreSQL and a Postgres-backed job queue. No Redis or Celery required. Runs on a single small server or a one-click cloud template.
