Common questions

Is it really free?

The self-hosted software is free, and seats, workspaces and channels are never metered. You still pay your own hosting and any external services you enable. The Agency and AI Intelligence require model credits; generated pictures require image-service credits. Supplied photos can be used without generating a new picture.

What does the Agency do?

It prepares brand content for human review, then delivers the approved content at the approved time to your connected accounts. An AI review or a brief confirmation does not approve publication. Enquiries and site visits can be recorded by your team; post engagement alone does not establish sales or qualified leads.

How does the AI budget work?

The Agency records provider-reported model cost when available and otherwise estimates usage from known prices. Failed requests and images can cost money. Calls already in progress, unreported charges and separately billed platform fees can exceed that estimate. Check the provider's balance and invoice for the actual charge; the budget pauses new work once recorded usage reaches the limit.

How long does it take to connect my accounts?

It depends on the platform. Bluesky, Mastodon and DEV.to connect in minutes. Meta (Facebook/Instagram/Threads), TikTok, LinkedIn and the others need an approved developer app first: self-hosters register their own (we document each step), and on the hosted service they appear as each platform approves ours.

What am I paying for on Managed Cloud?

Convenience: we host and update the app so you don't run servers. It's free during early access; we'll publish service levels and pricing, with notice, before anything is charged. The code stays open-source.

Can I move between self-hosted and managed?

It's the same open-source app on both, so nothing you rely on is proprietary. Moving an existing workspace between instances is a database migration we'll help with today; a self-serve export is on the roadmap.

Which platforms are supported?

Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky, Google Business, Mastodon, DEV.to and X — via each platform's official API. X publishes only: its API is pay-per-use, billed to the developer account behind the app.
