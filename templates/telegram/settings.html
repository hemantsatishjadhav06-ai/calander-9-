What you can say

In your paired chat, plain text is an instruction: say what you want posted and the team brings you a brief to confirm, then the draft to approve.

/brief An idea
Start a post.
/brainstorm An idea
Think it through first.
/reference
Send a photo, PDF or short text to use.
/status
What is happening in this chat.
{% if internal %}
/approvals · /post <ID>
Posts waiting for you, with their review card.
/week · /week prepare
This week's plan; prepare next week.
/pause · /resume · /retry
Your latest task.
/mute · /unmute
Messages from the team; the morning briefing still arrives.
{% else %}
/week
What is planned for you this week.
{% endif %}

Nothing publishes without your approval, and a plain “yes” never approves anything. In a group, use commands or mention the bot; send /help for everything.
