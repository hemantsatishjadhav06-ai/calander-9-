4. Connect a channel for status updates (optional)

A channel broadcasts brief draft, review, scheduling and delivery status updates to its subscribers. Questions, answers and approval buttons stay in your personally linked private chat or the appropriate client or team group.

  1. Link your own Telegram account above and press Start. Use an account that is a channel administrator.
  2. In the channel's administrator settings, add @{{ connection.bot_username|default:'your client bot' }} and enable Post Messages.
  3. Enter the channel's public @username or its private numeric channel ID. Confirm that its subscribers may see these status updates, then verify it below.

We verify both administrator identities. Telegram cannot list every passive subscriber; you control that audience. Private conversation content, post captions, internal costs and approval buttons are never broadcast here. Use a separate channel for each client workspace.
