{% comment %} An inline confirm (spec D8; DESIGN_SYSTEM.md 10.9): a destructive or paid act confirms where it is, never in a modal, an hx-confirm or the browser's confirm(). The act's button opens a group under it, named by the words of what it removes or starts, with Cancel and the confirm, which repeats the act. Opening moves focus to Cancel, so the words are read first; Cancel and Escape close it and put focus back on the act (Escape stops there, so an enclosing dialog stays open). Context: confirm_id a unique id: the group's, and "-words" for its words. label the act's words on its button ("Delete"). words one sentence: what it removes or starts ("Delete the routine Morning post? It stops running."). It names the group. confirm_label the confirm's words (default: label); they repeat the act ("Delete routine"). tone "paid" for an act that starts the team's work (secondary, then a tinted confirm); anything else is destructive (danger ghost, then the danger confirm). action a URL: the confirm submits a POST form there; or hx_post a URL the confirm posts to with htmx, with hx_target and hx_swap. testid "" on the act, "-region", "-cancel" and "-confirm" on the rest. {% endcomment %}
