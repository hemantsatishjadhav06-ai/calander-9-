{% comment %} The New post sheet (Phase 3, screen 1; docs/specs/03-rebuild-screens.md, "Screen 1"): one dialog with three ways to start a post. Home and Calendar include it for whoever may start posts (apps.studio.views.can_start_posts), beside components/primary_action.html labelled New post, which sends new-post:open; #new-post opens it on load. static/js/new-post-sheet.js (newPostSheet(), loaded deferred before Alpine on those two pages only) runs it inside the shell's Alpine scope: opening closes the phone drawer first, remembers the opener and puts focus in the mode's first field; Escape, the close button and the scrim close it and return focus to the opener; what was typed stays until the page goes; body.has-sheet while it is open. Below 1024px a bottom sheet (rounded top, at most 92vh, a grabber that is only a cue); from 1024px a 480px drawer on the right. It enters and leaves on .motion-sheet-* (styles.css: in place under reduced motion), and the scrim fades on the same tokens. Light only until its pages are declared (T22). The heading names the dialog through a static aria-labelledby (the a11y sweep reads only static labels); its words follow the view: "New post", or "New idea" for the idea form. Layout: the dialog is the scroller. Its header sticks to the top and each panel's decision bar to the bottom. A panel (data-view: team, self, idea; one view each for the one-primary counter) is display: contents, so its body (order-1), the foot links (order-2) and its bar (order-3) line up in the dialog's column while each panel stays one element. The overlay never blocks the page while closed (pointer-events-none; only its open children take events), so the scrim and the dialog can each run their own leave transition. Context: workspace, form (apps.studio.forms.BriefForm), features. {% endcomment %}
