# Guided post creation and delivery in the Agency thread

Updated 12 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata. These changes are prepared locally on `codex/openrouter-agency-readiness`, based on main `05ca828`; deployment to Railway has not been verified.

The thread is the place to brief, discuss, confirm, revise and inspect delivery of a post. **Confirm brief & create draft** authorizes creative preparation. Only a person's **Approve & schedule** authorizes delivery of the finished content, destination and time. An AI review verdict never grants publication approval.

## Prepare one useful campaign

1. Follow the activation checklist: **Verified facts**, an active brand account, a confirmed creative brief, human draft review and a successful native publication. Saved account health and configuration are not independent live verification.
2. Under **Agency → Verified facts**, record current claims with their source, expiry and human verifier. Verify current contacts, availability, price/discount, launch dates and registration/title claims before using them. Legacy Brand profile facts are unverified background notes. A removed/deactivated verifier or lost verification permission makes the claim unavailable until a permitted person verifies it again.
3. For a named-property campaign, attest the authentic uploaded image to its exact file and project under Verified facts. A replaced file/version invalidates that attestation. Generated images stay **illustrative**; an unverified image is not proof of a named project. The image actually used receives a code-owned AI-generated or unverified-photo caption disclosure in full, shortened and channel-specific text before final review. A current authentic-photo attestation or no picture has no such note. The app records a person's attestation, not an external property verification.
4. Use **Start guided post** on the Agency overview, or **Ask the team**. Describe the post—for example, “Invite families to arrange a site visit; ask what you need.” The account manager asks up to three focused questions at a time and remembers the answers in the same conversation. The compatibility creation route also enters this flow.
5. Under **Photo and creative direction**, choose a current workspace photo or upload JPEG/PNG/WebP, and choose the brand series or a fresh layout within the brand. A photo here is the image to publish. Design inspiration belongs in the reference description or Creative memory.
6. Review the topic, goal, audience, message, CTA, destinations, visual direction and source warnings shown before confirmation. Inspect the evidence, verified claims and image provenance. Tick **I checked the brief, source facts and account choices**, then use **Confirm brief & create draft**. An ordinary “yes,” a model response or another person's snapshot cannot authorize it.
7. Watch the actual caption, platform versions, graphic, revision and stage in the same thread. Essential text loss or unresolved source/final-payload review blocks handoff. A person reviews aesthetics, factual accuracy and the useful next step.
8. Use **Review draft and approval status** to approve the finished revision and choose a posting time. **Approve** alone leaves an approved post unscheduled. **Publish now** queues approved delivery; it is not a one-minute guarantee.

The internal staff workflow has these source/intake controls. Autopilot prepares drafts through its planning workflow; every finished post still needs human approval. Client portal access retains its own sign-off and visibility boundaries; staff-only source, readiness, cost and recovery controls are not exposed there.

## Source evidence and revisions

Each new guided creation collects the saved brand website and selected connected account evidence. The collector reads bounded visible HTML and up to five recent native posts for Facebook Pages, Facebook-backed Instagram and Instagram Login. Unsupported, expired or unavailable connections show explicitly labeled stored successful publications. It reads no private inbox messages or internal post notes for this source snapshot.

Website/caption excerpts are untrusted evidence and do not become approved brand claims automatically. Source cards show age and availability; stale/missing times, unavailable websites and stored-history fallbacks are visible warnings. Reads use only the saved brand URL with public DNS validation, connection pinning, verified TLS, bounded time/bytes and no unchecked redirect/pagination. Redirecting, compressed, inaccessible or script-only pages can be unavailable: use a readable direct URL or a person's source-backed claim. Missing verified claims make the prompts ask the owner; these controls are not a semantic proof of every generated sentence. A person still checks the final facts.

Evidence older than 30 minutes is refreshed and shown for another confirmation. Changed saved destination identity invalidates an old selection. The app checks local identity metadata; the pilot must independently check the real brand account and native permissions.

For a revision, select **Draft to discuss or revise** when needed, choose photo B to replace photo A, select **No supplied photo** to clear it, or keep the current choice. Brand/fresh style changes are stored with that revision and acknowledged by the team. A request for a new generated picture clears the old supplied-photo choice when appropriate. Current workspace ownership, permissions, source claims and image provenance are checked again. An approved/scheduled/published or unresolved-delivery draft cannot be silently revised around the human workflow.

A changed/expired claim or changed attested file cannot grant continued approval to the old source snapshot. Have a current permitted person verify the source, then request a fresh revision and review the final content again. Source/photo/style changes never authorize delivery.

## Inspect delivery and recover safely

Each same-thread draft card includes per-account posting status, revision, account/handle, posting time and available native post ID/link. An approved brief may still have unscheduled, pending, failed or held destinations; inspect each receipt.

| Receipt state | Staff action |
|---|---|
| Awaiting review / approved but unscheduled | Review the exact content and choose a future posting time through the existing approval page. |
| Scheduled / sending or awaiting confirmation | Leave the active attempt in place and inspect the account/status when due. Model work can delay the worker. |
| Delivery outcome unknown | Check the intended account before any further send. If the exact post exists, an authorized approver can record its native ID and public platform URL. This records a **human account inspection**, not an independent provider lookup, and sends nothing. |
| Checked absent | An authorized approver records the account check and notes, returning the content for fresh human review. It is not an automatic retry. Check relevance, choose a new future time and approve again. |
| Expired — needs review | The permitted lateness window has passed. Return the content for human review, update facts if necessary and approve a new future time. An expired unsent attempt cannot be marked as an existing publication. |
| Saved destination changed | Inspect the original native account with the owner. A changed destination cannot inherit the previous recovery decision. |

Recovery forms are bound to the current attempt, destination and content fingerprint. A stale form or changed permissions cannot grant a recovery action. Durable parent-post holds survive edits and removal/readdition of destination rows; use the explicit account-check workflow rather than recreating an attempt to bypass its hold.

## Coverage, reporting and costs

Choose a lead and different backup under Autopilot. Reminder checks run every 15 minutes: the lead's deadline window is one hour and the backup's is 30 minutes. Overdue review is escalated; nothing is approved or scheduled by a reminder. Live notification receipt depends on current membership, preferences, bounded alert volume and functioning worker/email delivery. Confirm it during the pilot.

Reports distinguish requested briefs, prepared drafts, approval/scheduled states, published, failed and blocked content, with failed work and paused planning notices. **Current backlog** is a separate snapshot taken when the report is written, including older outstanding content. Historical status changes are not reconstructed, and a post can occupy several channel states. A failed paid narrator or paused budget still leaves a recorded-count report.

Creative memory separates manually chosen designer references from measured performers. A ranking requires successful analytics captured within seven days and at least five measured posts on the same account, with posts at least three days old. Measurement dates and missing-evidence explanations stay visible. References remain useful when analytics are missing or stale.

Under **Outcomes**, a permitted staff member can record campaign/date/event totals after checking the existing inbox or CRM: enquiries, qualified enquiries and completed site visits, optionally linked to approved/published content. Keep personal lead names/contact details in that existing system. Entries are manually attributed, date-level aggregate events; they do not deduplicate people, infer attribution or calculate a conversion funnel. Repeat submission of the same form does not create a second entry.

Agency Studio consumes model credits and generated illustrations consume image-service credits. Supplied photos avoid a new image-generation request. Usage combines valid provider-reported cost with estimates; failures can cost money. The monthly budget pauses new work at a recorded threshold, but in-flight/unreported charges and separately billed services can exceed it. Check provider billing for actual charges. Support during early access is not a guaranteed emergency campaign-monitoring service.

## Graphics and pilot acceptance

The art director receives current source/photo/style choices, prior brand direction and curated examples. Prompts preserve authentic imagery and request crop-safe composition, readable type, one clear visual idea and useful negative space. Typography is rendered separately from the generated wordless illustration. Final per-account captions/comments and the graphic require exact-payload review; essential truncation/omission/overflow holds the post for correction. Large-image decoding is bounded, and missing image-service access produces an explicit supplied-photo or brand-background path.

These controls cannot certify premium aesthetics or property truth. Compare real owner-approved project/site-visit, educational and listing campaigns against the designer's best work, using phone/keyboard review and the intended platform variants. The synthetic renderer rehearsal is not an actual project photograph or paid model sample.
